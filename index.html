What we do

Commodity fumigation, port to port.

International Fumigators, Inc. protects grain, feed and other bulk commodities in transit and in storage — on vessels, at terminals and in warehouses.

Vessel Fumigation

Fumigation of ships and barges in port — holds, decks and cargo spaces treated to standard before sailing.

Export Fumigation

Pre-shipment fumigation for export cargo so your commodity clears inspection at its destination port.

USDA – PPQ Fumigation

Fumigation work performed to U.S. Department of Agriculture Plant Protection and Quarantine requirements.

Stored-Product Fumigation

Warehouses, silos and bagged goods — bulk and packaged commodities protected from stored-product pests.

Grain & Silo Fumigation

On-farm and terminal grain storage treated to keep harvested commodities pest-free.
